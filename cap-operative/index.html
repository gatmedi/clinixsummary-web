Operative Notes

Operative & Procedural Notes. Automatically.

Surgical documentation is time‑critical and detail‑intensive. ClinixSummary captures operative narration — whether dictated during or immediately after a procedure — and transforms it into structured operative reports that meet regulatory and billing requirements.

mic

Surgical Narration Capture

Dictate your operative narrative naturally. Our models parse surgical terminology, anatomical references, instrument names and technique descriptions with high fidelity.

summarize

Structured Operative Reports

Automatically structure findings, procedure steps, specimens, complications and post‑operative orders into compliant operative report formats.

draft

Procedure-Specific Templates

Leverage procedure‑specific templates for common surgeries — from laparoscopic cholecystectomy to total joint replacement — pre‑populated with expected fields.

receipt_long

Billing-Ready Documentation

Generate documentation that supports accurate CPT coding and medical necessity justification, reducing post‑operative billing delays.

headphones

Auto Ambient Dictate — Operative Mode

ClinixSummary’s Auto Ambient Dictate feature includes a dedicated operative mode that automatically detects surgical narration, distinguishes it from team communication and background noise, and begins structured documentation capture without manual activation. Dictate naturally while you operate — your report is waiting when you’re done.
